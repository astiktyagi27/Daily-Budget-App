### 2.3 Notes for the Project Manager / Product Manager / Designer
1. **Design numbers do not add up (F-014 / `month-statement.md` §8.1, used by TC-013-41 and F-014).** The "so far on 20 Nov" example shows Period 2 Spent ₹4,000, Saved ₹456, Overspent ₹291. The 13 – 20 Nov budget is 4 × 55,223 + 4 × 55,222 = ₹4,417.80, so budget − net spent = ₹417.80, which is not 456 − 291 = ₹165 (and the 20 Nov rule D-060 makes today count only in Overspent). The Tester's seed M20 (14 whole-rupee days, listed above) gives Spent ₹4,000, Saved ₹715, Overspent ₹297, Left ₹6,900 and every line adds up. **Please correct §8.1 or confirm M20.**
2. **Bar length basis (AC-4).** "Proportional to its paise" does not say whether the longest bar fills the track (relative to the largest row) or each bar equals its share of the total. TC-013-06 asserts only the **ratio between bars** (Shopping : Other = 1,00,000 : 3,91,000, within one decimal place) and that no bar exceeds the track. Please state the basis.
3. **A day with only refunds in One day (TC-013-45):** F-013 §2.2 has the empty sentence for "no spends in the range" and §2.6 shows refund lines under a sentence. The case where both apply is not written.
4. **Highest day when the largest ended net spend is ≤ ₹0 (TC-013-46):** the spec covers "all ₹0" (`No spends yet`) but not "no spends but a refund day". Proposed: treat it as `No spends yet`.
5. **Range choice across restarts (TC-013-42, -52):** not specified. Proposed: kept while the app is open (as F-015 filters), reset at restart.
6. **"incl. n covered from savings" when a day is only partly covered (TC-013-41).** F-013 §2.3 counts a fully covered day as on budget and a partly covered day as overspent, but does not say whether a partly covered day is part of the `incl. <n> covered from savings` count. In M20, 6 Nov is covered 20,000 of 44,000.

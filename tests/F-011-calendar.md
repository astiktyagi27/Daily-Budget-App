## 0. How to run these cases

**Environment and shared definitions:** as F-010 §0 (data sets **A13**, **A13c300**, **S8**, **M20** (the canonical shared seed, `phase3_fixtures` `m20`), **F12**, the harness list and the harness additions) which in turn uses F-002 §0 (**T5**, **Small**, **X1/X2**, **Overflow check**), F-007 §0 (**[MEM]**, **[EMU]**, **[FAKE]**), F-008 §0 (**Today(d)**, **Fake repos**, `findCopy`) and F-009 §0. The real F-006 engine is used. The calendar is tested **on Home** (`pumpHome`) and, for the grid maths, as pure functions.

**Additional definitions**

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OCT-NOV** | Two stored months for past-month cases: **Oct** (salary day 5, 5 Oct – 4 Nov 2026, 31 days, E2 values: 80,323 for the first 18 days, 80,322 for the other 13; spends 5 Oct 50,000 and 6 Oct 1,00,000) and **Nov** = A13. Oct is stored first (`addMonth(store, month: oct2026)`), then Nov. **Today(13 Nov 2026)**. 5 Oct is a Monday: 5 Oct saved 30,323 → `₹303`; 6 Oct overspent 19,677 → `₹196`; a day without spends reads `nothing spent, saved ₹803` |
| **Grid layout function** | The pure function behind the grid (proposed `calendarGrid(month, firstBudgetedDay)`): returns the cells Monday to Sunday, each `outside` / `notBudgeted` / `budgeted(date)`. Tested without widgets in `test/domain/budget/calendar_grid_test.dart` |
| **Cell view-model** | The pure mapping `DayResult` (+ today, change days, cover, refunds) → cell kind, mark, fill token, amount text, semantics label (proposed `calendarCell(...)`) |
| **Short form** | The pure amount short-form function (`formatRupeesShort(Paise)` in `core/format/money_format.dart`, plan T-321) used by cells only |
| **Cell finder** | A day cell is found by its semantics label (`find.bySemanticsLabel('Thursday 5 November, spent ₹500, saved ₹330')`) or by the plan's `ValueKey('day-2026-11-05')`; marks by their widget key (`CalendarMark.saved`, `.onBudget`, `.overspent`) |
| **Grey-scale** | A `ColorFiltered` widget with the saturation-0 matrix around the grid; marks are compared by their shape widget (not by colour), and the rendered luminance of the three fills is compared by `RepaintBoundary.toImage` pixel sampling |
| **Calendar states (P3)** | For sweeps (shared with F-010 TC-010-40): A13; A13c300; S8; M20; F12; OCT-NOV on the past month (Oct); new month not set up (last month); loading grid; past-month load error; 6-row month (M20); 4-row month (Feb 2027); a day with 50 spends; ₹10 crore day. 12 states |

### 2.3 Notes for the Project Manager / Product Manager / Designer
1. **AC-2 says "30 Jan – 28 Feb 2028 … 30 budget days including 29 Feb". It does not include 29 Feb.** F-006 §2.1 makes 29 Feb the **first day of the next month** (29 Feb – 29 Mar 2028). Salary day 30 in 2028 therefore gives two months, both 30 days: 30 Jan – 28 Feb (no leap day) and 29 Feb – 29 Mar (leap day first). TC-011-05 and -06 test the F-006 truth; please correct the AC wording.
2. **Short-form edge cases** (TC-011-24): the spec gives `₹12k`, `₹1.5L`, `₹10Cr` ("one decimal, rounded down"). Not stated: whether exactly ₹1 lakh / ₹1 crore reads `₹1L` / `₹1Cr` or `₹1.0L` / `₹1.0Cr` (the spec's `₹10Cr` suggests the `.0` is dropped), whether ₹99,999 is `₹99k`, and how a negative net spend such as −₹12,000 reads (`−₹12k` proposed). **Settled at Gate 1 (D-073): `₹1L`, `₹1Cr`, `₹99k`, `−₹12k`, `.0` dropped; TC-011-24 follows the design table.**
3. **The first of the calendar month** (AC-1: "the 1st of the new calendar month shows `1 Dec`"): in a month that starts on the 1st (M20, 1 Dec 2026) the first cell is the 1st too. The design preview shows a plain `1`. TC-011-41 assumes plain `1` (no change of month inside the grid). **Settled at Gate 1 (D-075 (2)): plain `1`.**
4. **AC-4 (grey-scale) is hard to automate.** The widget test proves the three marks are different shapes and the labels carry the status; "told apart in grey" by eye is the manual case TC-011-12.
5. **AC-18 timing** is meaningful only on the emulator (TC-011-37, worst of 5).
6. **Harness additions** needed (F-010 §0 list plus): `readFailsFor(monthId)` on `FakeMoneyStore` for TC-011-50.
7. **Cell semantics wording for refund days:** F-011 §5 writes `net <x> after refunds`, with `−` for negatives, and the design `net minus ₹1,300`; the test uses the design (screen readers say "minus", AC-10).

8. **Plan cross-check (2026-11-16):** every AC has a plan task and at least one case. **The plan's T-320 repeats the AC-2 error** ("30 Jan to 28 Feb 2028 (29 Feb)"): a Developer would look for 29 Feb in a month that ends on 28 Feb; see note 1. The plan's open question 2 asks whether the **short money forms** need an Owner decision (they change how an amount is displayed, with no D-number): if the Owner drops them, TC-011-23 and -24 change to the full-amount rule and AC-11 goes. Plan T-322 (`watchMonthIndex`, an additive change to the finished F-007 repository) has its own `month_index_test.dart`, which now has its own cases TC-011-59 to -66 (added at T-309, D-078); TC-011-43 and -29 exercise it through the screen.
